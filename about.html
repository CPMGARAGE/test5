THE STORY SO FAR

Long before the events of TerminalTanks, the world had already faced a crisis like this one.

Resources were running out, and people began fighting over what remained. The most powerful among them became obsessed with what they could take and keep. They gathered enormous reserves, built armies around them, and refused to let anyone else have what they had collected.

They became known as the Ancient Hoarders. No army could defeat them, and the world was running out of time.

THE FIRST CRISIS

One person decided to fight back. A powerful mage gathered the last great reserves of magic and created a spell capable of imprisoning the Hoarders.

But the spell required more power than the mage could survive giving. With no other way to save the world, the mage sacrificed every last ounce of energy in their body and completed the spell.

The Ancient Hoarders were sealed away. The world survived.

The mage did not.

The prisons became known as Terminal Tickets. Over generations, their true purpose was forgotten. They became ancient artifacts whose origins were almost completely lost to history.

THE WORLD FORGOT

Centuries passed. The story of the mage became legend, and the Terminal Tickets became curiosities. Almost nobody remembered that each one contained something still alive inside.

Then history began to repeat itself.

THE SECOND RESOURCE CRISIS

All was well in the world of TerminalTanks, until people started fighting over resources. The fighting made people greedy, and the greed drained the world.

The land dried up, and the richest people alive began hoarding what was left. They built their own armies, fortresses and machines around their wealth. These new hoarders became the bosses.

The Master was the biggest and grandest of them all. He controlled more resources than anyone else and built the Master's Citadel to protect them.

THE ROAD TO THE MASTER

You come to put it right. The Tournament is your road through the hoarders: twenty duels that begin at Boot Camp and end at the Master's Citadel.

Every greedy boss you take down makes you stronger, and the resources they sat on are reclaimed and returned to humanity.

At the end of the Tournament, you defeat the Master and secure the greatest remaining stockpile of resources in the world.

For the first time in years, the world's wealth is no longer controlled by one person.

But your victory is noticed far beyond your world.

THE ALIENS

Another world was running dry too. Its people had heard about the Master's enormous wealth and came to take it for themselves.

They arrived too late.

You had already defeated the Master and secured the riches. Their target changed.

It is you now.

THE INVASION

The missions are that war. The aliens want everything you took back from the hoarders, and they want it for their own planet.

Your job is to keep it safe. Hunt the invaders, survive their increasingly powerful forces, and fight the UFOs as they grow from lone scouts into the Archon.

Finally, the Mothership arrives with two drones. It is the greatest assault yet, and it will require everything you have learned.

Defeat the Mothership, and the invasion ends. The riches can finally be restored to the world.

THE TERMINAL TICKETS

But the oldest threat was never destroyed.

The Terminal Tickets were not treasures. They were prisons.

The ten most powerful Ancient Hoarders from the First Crisis were never defeated. The mage could not destroy them, so they were bound inside magical prisons and sealed away.

The mage gave their life to create those prisons. For generations, the seals held.

Now the artifacts have been rediscovered.

Each Terminal Ticket contains one of the ancient bosses. When a Ticket is activated, its seal is broken and the imprisoned Hoarder is released.

You must fight them where they were sealed, using whatever equipment is available to you. Each boss has its own tank, battlefield and ammunition, and each battle is different from the last.

These are not ordinary bosses. They are the enemies the ancient mage could only imprison.

Now you have the chance to finish what they started.

A defeated Terminal Ticket boss never returns. Each victory finally ends the threat that the ancient mage sacrificed everything to contain.

There are ten. When the last one falls, the Terminal Tickets are gone for good.

HOW IT IS BUILT

TerminalTanks is one Python file of about 10,000 lines. It needs Python 3.10 or newer and a UTF-8 terminal of at least 96 by 28 characters. It uses no third-party packages: drawing, input and animation are all built on the standard library and plain ANSI escape sequences.

Rendering ANSI escape sequences, no curses Multiplayer LAN play over TCP, with UDP discovery so hosts appear on their own Fairness The simulation is seeded and deterministic, and visual effects use their own random generator so they cannot change an outcome Saves One small JSON file, versioned, with migration for older saves and room for fields from newer ones In the browser The same game file on the Pyodide Python runtime, drawn by a hand-written canvas renderer

The deterministic design is what makes LAN play work: both machines run the same simulation from the same starting setup, so a shot only needs to send its angle and power. It also keeps matches reproducible.

HOW THE BROWSER VERSION WORKS

The browser version runs the same terminaltanks.py as the terminal, but not literally untouched. The page supplies stand-ins for the operating-system terminal modules the game imports and paces the game one frame at a time. Scenes, physics, AI and saves are the same code in both places.

The page draws the game with its own canvas renderer, with no terminal library and no other third-party code. The one outside piece is Pyodide, the Python runtime itself, which downloads on your first visit (about 10 MB) and is cached after that. The game and its renderer still follow the project's rule of no dependencies of their own.

Saves have changed shape over time, and the game still reads every older version and converts it. For example, the old Master fight at level 5 now lives at level 20, and its rewards carry over. Custom tanks are stored by stable part names rather than positions in a list, so a tank you build today still loads after new parts are added, and a part that is ever removed falls back to a safe default instead of breaking the save.

TANKS OF YOUR OWN

The Workshop lets you assemble a tank from parts you have unlocked: a turret, hull, tracks, barrel, glow, ambient effect and accent color. Name it, give it a line of lore, preview it, and equip it. Saving a design costs coins, and you can keep up to of them. Official tanks are never overwritten.

TERMINAL TICKETS

A Terminal Ticket is the most expensive thing in the shop, but it is more than a purchase. Each Ticket is an ancient magical prison left behind by the mage of the First Crisis.

Buying one gives you access to one of ten imprisoned Ancient Hoarders. The boss is selected from the remaining seals, and each has its own tank, map and ammunition.

The seal does not disappear until the boss is defeated. Once you win, that ancient Hoarder is permanently removed from the pool and you keep the rewards, including the boss's equipment and home screen.

A defeated boss can never appear again. Once all ten Ancient Hoarders have been defeated, the Terminal Tickets are finally exhausted.
